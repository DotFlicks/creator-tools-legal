Data Deletion Instructions

dot.flicks Creator Tools · Last updated: 21 November 2026

dot.flicks Creator Tools stores data only on the computer of the owner of @dot.flicks (see the Privacy Policy). If you commented on a @dot.flicks post or received a message from it, the stored data about you is at most: your Instagram username, your Instagram-scoped ID, your comment text and time, and a record that one message was sent to you.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a direct message to @dot.flicks on Instagram saying "Delete my data".
  2. All stored data about your account is deleted within 30 days, and you get a reply confirming it.
  3. You are also added to a do-not-message list, so the app never messages you again. Only a one-way (hashed) form of your ID is kept for that purpose.

Automatic deletion

Even without a request, commenter information is deleted automatically within 30 days.
