Privacy Policy

dot.flicks Creator Tools · Last updated: 21 November 2026

dot.flicks Creator Tools ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by the owner of the Instagram account @dot.flicks to analyse that account and to reply to people who comment on its posts. It is not offered to the public. It connects to Instagram only through Meta's official Instagram API.

What data the app processes

  • The owner's own account data: profile details, posts and Reels, and their statistics (for example views, likes, comments, shares, saves and watch time), as provided by Meta's API.
  • Comments on the owner's posts: the comment text, the time it was posted, and the commenter's Instagram username and Instagram-scoped ID.
  • Replies sent by the app: when someone comments a keyword the owner asked for (for example "comment REPO and I'll send you the link"), the app sends that person one private message. The app records that the message was sent and Meta's message ID, not the contents of any conversation.

How the data is used

Only to show the owner how their content performs and to send the one message a commenter asked for. The data is never sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to contact anyone who did not comment the requested keyword.

Where the data is stored

Only on the owner's own computer. There is no app server and no cloud database. The Instagram access token is stored encrypted, with the key kept in the operating system's credential store.

How long the data is kept

Commenter information (username, ID, comment text) is deleted within 30 days. The owner's own account statistics are kept until the owner deletes them.

Your choices and deletion

  • Reply STOP to a message from @dot.flicks and you will not receive another automated message.
  • To have your data deleted, follow the data deletion instructions.

Contact

Send a direct message to @dot.flicks on Instagram.
